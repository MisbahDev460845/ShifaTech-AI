# 🩺 ShifaTech AI

### AI-Powered Healthcare Guidance in Urdu & English

ShifaTech AI is a modern AI-powered healthcare triage platform designed to help users understand symptoms and medical documents through accessible AI-assisted guidance.

The platform supports both **English and Urdu** and is designed with a focus on accessibility, simplicity, privacy, and social impact.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🩺 AI Symptom Checker
Users can describe their symptoms and receive an AI-assisted preliminary risk assessment.

### 🎙️ Voice Symptoms
Users can describe symptoms using voice in English or Urdu.

### 📄 Medical Report Scanner
Upload medical reports and use AI-powered document analysis to extract important information.

### 💊 Prescription Reader
Analyze readable information from handwritten prescriptions using AI vision.

### 📋 Medical Summary
Generate simple, structured summaries from symptoms and uploaded medical information.

### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Summary
Generate an organized AI-assisted summary that can be reviewed by a qualified healthcare professional.

### 🚨 Risk Assessment
The system categorizes situations into:

- LOW
- MODERATE
- HIGH
- EMERGENCY

### 🌐 Bilingual Interface
Full support for:

- English
- اردو

The Urdu interface uses RTL layout.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Many people, especially in underserved and rural communities, face difficulties accessing healthcare information.

Common challenges include:

- Limited access to healthcare professionals
- Language barriers
- Difficulty understanding medical reports
- Difficult-to-read handwritten prescriptions
- Uncertainty about the urgency of symptoms

---

## 💡 Solution

ShifaTech AI combines AI-powered text, voice, vision, and document analysis to provide understandable healthcare information and preliminary triage guidance.

The goal is to help users better understand their information and identify appropriate next steps.

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

ShifaTech AI is designed with privacy and security in mind.

Sensitive API keys should be stored using secure server-side secrets and should never be exposed in frontend code.

Users should only upload medical information they have permission to share.

---

## ⚠️ Medical Disclaimer

ShifaTech AI is an AI-assisted healthcare information and triage platform.

It does **not** provide definitive medical diagnoses and does not replace a qualified doctor or other healthcare professional.

AI-generated information should be verified by a qualified healthcare professional.

For emergencies, users should contact local emergency medical services or seek urgent medical attention.
